Biography

A boy from Madeira

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. From a young age, he showed an extraordinary passion for football. His talent, determination and strong work ethic helped him rise from the streets of Madeira to become one of the greatest footballers in history.

Ronaldo's journey is a true example of how discipline, self-belief and relentless hard work can turn dreams into reality.

Spotted by Sporting CP at twelve, he left his island home alone for Lisbon, overcame a heart condition at fifteen, and signed for Manchester United in 2003 — where he won his first Ballon d'Or and became a global star under Sir Alex Ferguson.

From a record £80m move to Real Madrid to Euro 2016 glory with Portugal and history at five World Cups, his story keeps inspiring millions. More about Cristiano Ronaldo...
